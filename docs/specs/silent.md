## 3. Batches (dependency order; 129 = 22 + 16 + 18 + 15 + 13 + 20 + 22 + 3)
### Batch 1: plain attacks, draw, block (22 cards)
Backstab, Slice, Skewer, DaggerSpray, FlickFlack, Ricochet, Dash, GrandFinale, EchoingSlash, Finisher, Flechettes, PreciseCut, Murder, Pinpoint, Deflect, Backflip, EscapePlan, Adrenaline, Expertise*, Tactician, Reflex, Untouchable (*needs G2; defer to batch 7 if C19 is late).
* Hit-count cards: `Skewer` is X-cost (`ResolveEnergyXValue()`, template `Cascade`); `Finisher` = Attack plays finished this turn by the owner (`C/Finisher.cs:23`, the played card is not counted yet); `Flechettes` = Skills in hand when played; `PreciseCut` and `Murder` use `CalculatedDamageVar` (hand size; `CardDrawnEntry` over the **whole combat** by the owner, `C/Murder.cs:22`). `Ricochet`/`FlickFlack` are Sly. `EchoingSlash` repeats on each kill (an `AttackContext` loop, `C/EchoingSlash.cs:25-33`). `Pinpoint` is `ReduceCostBy(skills played this turn)` in `AfterCardEnteredCombat` and `-1` per Skill played (`C/Pinpoint.cs`), copy `content/cards/flatten.rs:98 reduce_cost`. `Backstab`/`Suppress`: Innate + Exhaust.
* Loadout/situations: Sly cards (`FlickFlack`, `Ricochet`, `Reflex`, `Tactician`, `Untouchable`) only fire when **discarded by a card effect**, so put `Survivor` (starter) in hand and discard them with it; they do nothing when flushed at end of turn. `GrandFinale` needs an empty draw pile (small deck: `deck` holds only the cards under test). `Murder` after several draws. `DaggerSpray`/`FlickFlack`/`EchoingSlash` and `Ricochet` need 2+ enemies (`slimes_normal`, `nibbits_normal`); a kill chain for `EchoingSlash` needs low-HP enemies (`slimes_weak`). `Ricochet` draws `CombatTargets` per hit.
* Encounters: none special.

### Batch 2: discard prompts, Sly and hand tricks (10 cards, 4 powers, 2 relics)
Acrobatics, DaggerThrow, Prepared, CalculatedGamble, HandTrick*, MementoMori, ToolsOfTheTrade + `ToolsOfTheTradePower`, MasterPlanner + `MasterPlannerPower`, Nightmare + `NightmarePower`, WellLaidPlans + `WellLaidPlansPower`; relics `Tingsha`, `ToughBandages`.
* Prompts: `FromHandForDiscard` (mandatory, `choose_cards` with the discard prompt), then `CardCmd.Discard` (`Acrobatics`/`Prepared`: draw first, then discard; `CalculatedGamble`: `DiscardAndDraw` with the **hand size**, `C/CalculatedGamble.cs`). `ToolsOfTheTradePower`: `ModifyHandDraw +Amount` and a discard prompt in `AfterPlayerTurnStart`. `WellLaidPlansPower` only vetoes `ShouldFlush` for its owner, so the whole hand is kept (like ported `retain_hand.rs`; `P/WellLaidPlansPower.cs`). `MasterPlanner` gives Sly to every Skill played (`ApplyKeyword`, `P/MasterPlannerPower.cs`). `Nightmare`: `FromHand` prompt, `SetSelectedCard` stores a clone with the affliction cleared in the power's **internal data**, `BeforeHandDraw` adds `Amount` clones to hand and removes itself (`P/NightmarePower.cs`).

### Batch 3: block, Dexterity, Intangible (10 cards, 7 powers, 1 potion)
Footwork, Anticipate + `AnticipatePower`, Sidestep, DodgeAndRoll, Abrasive, PiercingWail + `PiercingWailPower`, Blur + `BlurPower`, Afterimage + `AfterimagePower`, Shadowmeld + `ShadowmeldPower`, WraithForm + `IntangiblePower` + `WraithFormPower`; potion `GhostInAJar` (Intangible 1).
* Temporary stat powers (`AnticipatePower` Dexterity, `PiercingWailPower` Strength loss on **all** enemies) follow `content/powers/temporary_strength.rs` and `EnfeeblingTouch`; `FadePower`/`HelicalDartPower` use the same shape. `Sidestep` uses ported `EnergyNextTurn`, `DodgeAndRoll` ported `BlockNextTurn`, `Abrasive` ported Thorns + Dexterity.
* `IntangiblePower` caps HP loss to 1 in `ModifyHpLostAfterOsty` **and** caps the damage in `ModifyDamageCap` (block loss and previews) and decrements at the enemy side turn end only (`P/IntangiblePower.cs`). `WraithFormPower` is a **debuff** that loses 1 Dexterity at every owner side-turn start (`AfterSideTurnStart`). `AfterimagePower`/`SerpentFormPower` store per-card amounts in internal data between `BeforeCardPlayed` and `AfterCardPlayed` (do not fire for the card that applied them; `P/AfterimagePower.cs`); Afterimage block is `Unpowered`. `BlurPower` makes `ShouldClearBlock` false and decrements at side-turn start. `ShadowmeldPower`: block x2^Amount, removed at side-turn end.
* Loadout: 2+ turns, an enemy that attacks every turn (any); `Piercing Wail` and Anticipate need an attacker to show the effect; Intangible with a multi-hit enemy (`slimes_normal`).

### Batch 4: tempo and energy (7 cards, 8 powers)
ShadowStep + `ShadowStepPower` + `DoubleDamagePower`, Pounce + `FreeSkillPower`, Predator + `DrawCardsNextTurnPower`, BulletTime + `NoDrawPower`, Burst + `BurstPower`, Speedster + `SpeedsterPower`, SerpentForm + `SerpentFormPower`.

## 4. Silent mechanics a porter must know
* **Poison tick order** (`P/PoisonPower.cs`): debuff, `Counter`. `AfterSideTurnStart` of the **owner's own side** (enemies: start of the enemy turn; a poisoned player at their own turn start): `TriggerCount = min(Amount, 1 + sum of living opponents' Accelerant)`. Per trigger: `CreatureCmd.Damage(owner, Amount, Unblockable | Unpowered, dealer None, card None)` (Strength/Vulnerable/block do not apply; `AfterDamageGiven` listeners see a dealer of `None`, so `Envenom` never retriggers), then `PowerCmd.Decrement` only if still alive. So one Poison of 3 ticks 3 then leaves 2; `Accelerant` re-ticks decreasing amounts. Poison on a creature that dies mid-loop skips the remaining ticks. Applying Poison is a debuff, so Artifact consumes a stack instead (`semantics/monsters.md:41`); the tick itself is damage and ignores Artifact.
* **Shivs:** a token with the Shiv tag, Exhaust, and an `AttackCommand` per `Shiv.OnPlay`; `Accuracy`, `Phantom Blades`, `KnifeTrap`, `HelicalDart` read the tag, not the id. A Shiv upgraded by `CardCmd.Upgrade` keeps +2 for the combat only.
* **Discard and Sly:** `CardCmd.Discard` moves cards to the discard pile, logs `CardDiscarded`, fires `AfterCardDiscarded`, **then** autoplays the Sly ones (`AutoPlayType.SlyDiscard`, `CardCmd.cs:172-209`); `DiscardAndDraw` draws between the hooks and the Sly autoplays. End-of-turn flush, exhaust and `Retain` do not trigger Sly. `IsSlyThisTurn` = Sly keyword or single-turn Sly (cleared by `EndOfTurnCleanup`).
* **Retain:** `ShouldRetainThisTurn` = Retain keyword or single-turn retain; `WellLaidPlans` (whole hand, via `ShouldFlush`), `Expertise` (cards it drew, single-turn), `Snakebite`/`PhantomBlades` (keyword).
* **Hand cap:** the hand holds 10 (`CardPileCmd.cs`); verify the over-cap path of `AddGeneratedCardsToCombat(Hand)` with `StormOfSteel`/`BladeDance` before relying on it.
* **Rng:** `BouncingFlask`, `Ricochet`, `SerpentForm`, `Tingsha`: `CombatTargets`; `Nightmare`: none; `BladeOfInk`/Shiv: none.
