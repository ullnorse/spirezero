# Spec: porting Defect's missing Act 1 content (S-DR)

Research only, no engine code. Source: `cargo xtask coverage --character Defect --act overgrowth --allow-missing --verbose` = 77 cards, 26 powers, 7 relics, 3 potions (113).
Two powers are multiplayer-only (below), so **111 to port**. Per-entity data: `cargo xtask inventory --character Defect <id>... [--deps]`. Paths under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`:
`C/` = `Models.Cards/`, `P/` = `Models.Powers/`, `R/` = `Models.Relics/`, `Po/` = `Models.Potions/`, `OC` = `Commands/OrbCmd.cs`. Engine paths under `crates/sts2-core/src/`.
Siblings: `orbs.md` (orb semantics, hooks, O1-O11), `ironclad.md` (same format), `hooks.md`. A `P` suffix = power. Ported already: `Zap`, `Dualcast`, `Coolheaded`, `Darkness`, `Fusion`,
`Glasswork`, `Chaos`, `Defragment`, `Capacitor`, `BulkUp`, `CrackedCore`, `Focus`, all five orbs (`combat/commands/orb.rs`, loadout `c16-orbs.json`).

## 3. Batches (dependency order; 111 = 19 + 20 + 18 + 22 + 19 + 13)
### Batch 1: orb channel and evoke cards, no new powers (19 cards)
BallLightning, ColdSnap, Chill, Tempest, Glacier, IceLance, MeteorStrike, ShadowShield, Null, Rainbow, Refract, Voltaic, Barrage, CompileDriver, TeslaCoil, MultiCast, Quadcast, Shatter, Modded.
* Counts: `Chill` 1 Frost per hittable enemy (`C/Chill.cs:29-34`); `Tempest` X (+1 upgraded); `Voltaic` = Lightning channelled this combat (`orbs_channeled_this_combat`); `Rainbow` L+F+D; `IceLance` 3 Frost, `Refract` 2 Glass, `MeteorStrike` 3 Plasma.
* `Barrage` hits = orb count, `CompileDriver` cards = **distinct orb kinds** (`GroupBy(Id)`), both read the live queue at `Calculate` time (`C/Barrage.cs`, `C/CompileDriver.cs`). `TeslaCoil`: damage, then raw `orb_passive` on every Lightning with the card's target. `MultiCast`/`Quadcast`/`Shatter`: evoke the front orb N times, dequeue on the last (`orbs.md` §3); `Shatter` reads the count after its damage.
* `Modded`: `add_orb_slots(Repeat)`, draw, `add_this_combat(+1)` own cost (`C/Modded.cs`).
* Loadout: queues of 1, 2, 3 and a full queue (`Capacitor`/`Modded` for slots); mixed kinds for `CompileDriver`/`Barrage`/`Rainbow`; Focus 0 first. 2-3 enemies (`slimes_normal`, `nibbits_normal`) for Lightning random targets and `Shatter`/`Chill`; an empty queue for the no-op paths (`MultiCast`, `Shatter` with nothing to evoke); end turns so passives fire.
* Special enemies: `Null` (Weak) on `cubex_construct_normal` (Artifact blocks it, `semantics/monsters.md:41`). Lightning passive/evoke on `inklets_normal` (Slippery: each loss >= 1 becomes 1, `P/SlipperyPower.cs`).

### Batch 2: Focus, temporary Focus, orb relics and potions (5 cards, 6 powers, 7 relics, 2 potions = 20)
FocusedStrike + `FocusedStrikePower`, Hotfix + `HotfixPower`, Synchronize + `SynchronizePower`, Hyperbeam + `HyperbeamFocusDownPower`, BiasedCognition + `BiasedCognitionPower`, first `TemporaryFocusPower`;
relics `DataDisk`, `RunicCapacitor`, `SymbioticVirus`, `GoldPlatedCables`, `Metronome`, `EmotionChip`; potions `FocusPotion`, `PotionOfCapacity`, `EssenceOfDarkness`.
* **Temporary Focus** (`P/TemporaryFocusPower.cs:130-150`): `BeforeApplied` applies `Sign * amount` of real Focus silently, `AfterPowerAmountChanged` re-applies the delta, owner's `AfterSideTurnEnd` removes it and applies the opposite; copy `content/powers/temporary_strength.rs`. `HyperbeamFocusDownPower` is the negative one (Hyperbeam: 24 damage to all, then **-3 Focus** applied after the attack, until turn end). `ITemporaryPower` is `PowerKind::is_temporary` (`powers/mod.rs:217`).
* `Synchronize`: Focus = distinct orb kinds x `CalculationExtra` at play time. `BiasedCognition`: +5 Focus, then `-Amount` at each of the owner's `AfterSideTurnStart`; Focus floors orb values at 0 (`focus.rs`) but the power itself goes negative (`AllowNegative`).
* Relics: `DataDisk` +1 Focus in `AfterRoomEntered` of a `CombatRoom`; `RunicCapacitor` +3 slots turn 1 (`AfterSideTurnStart`); `SymbioticVirus` 1 Dark turn 1; `GoldPlatedCables` +1 trigger for `Orbs[0]` only; `Metronome` counts channels per combat (reset `AfterRoomEntered` in a `CombatRoom`), 30 Unpowered damage to all enemies at the 7th (`R/Metronome.cs:44-96`); `EmotionChip` calls `Passive(countAffectedByHooks: true)` (so `GoldPlatedCables` counts) on every orb at `AfterPlayerTurnStart` if HP was lost in the previous turn.
* Loadout: Focus from 0 to 3+ and negative Focus (`BiasedCognition` twice, then run 3 turns); `Hyperbeam` with orbs queued so the end-of-turn passive sees -3; `GoldPlatedCables` with a Lightning in front and a Frost behind (only `Orbs[0]` doubles); `EmotionChip` with enemy damage taken, then none; `Metronome` with 7+ channels (`Chaos`/`Rainbow`/`Capacitor`); `PotionOfCapacity` before a full queue; `EssenceOfDarkness` at capacity 3 and 6.

### Batch 3: orb powers (9 cards, 9 powers = 18)
Loop + `LoopPower`, Storm + `StormPower`, LightningRod + `LightningRodPower`, Spinner + `SpinnerPower`, Coolant + `CoolantPower`, Hailstorm + `HailstormPower`, ConsumingShadow + `ConsumingShadowPower`, Thunder + `ThunderPower`, TrashToTreasure + `TrashToTreasurePower`.
* Timing (`orbs.md` §5, §7): `Loop` raw Passive of `Orbs[0]` at `AfterPlayerTurnStart`; `Storm` snapshots `Amount` at `BeforeCardPlayed`, channels in `AfterCardPlayed`, only for Power cards; `LightningRod` and `Spinner` at `AfterEnergyReset`; `Coolant` block = distinct kinds x `Amount`; `Hailstorm` at `BeforeSideTurnEnd` (before the orb passives) if >= 1 Frost; `ConsumingShadow` evokes `EvokeLast` at `AfterSideTurnEnd`; `Thunder` on the owner's Lightning evoke damages each target (`AfterOrbEvoked`); `TrashToTreasure` on a Status the owner generated draws `random_orb` (`CombatOrbs`).
* Loadout: `Storm` + Power cards; `Loop` with Dark/Glass front orb; `ConsumingShadow` with 1 vs several orbs; `Thunder` + `Zap` evoke (Dualcast) with 2 enemies; `TrashToTreasure` needs a Status generator (`BoostAway`, `GunkUp`: batch 4), so add that case to batch 4's loadout.
* Special enemies: `Thunder`/`Hailstorm`/`Spinner` multi-target damage on `inklets_normal` (Slippery) and `fogmog_normal` (Illusion: `EyeWithTeeth` spawns mid-fight and changes the hittable list for Lightning/Dark, `semantics/monsters.md:47-56`).

### Batch 4: attacks, block, statuses, no new powers (22 cards)
AdaptiveStrike, AllForOne, BeamCell, BoostAway, BootSequence, Burn, Void, Compact, Fuel, ChargeBattery, Claw, DoubleEnergy, FightThrough, FlakCannon, Ftl, GoForTheEyes, GunkUp, HelixDrill, Hologram, Leap, MomentumStrike, Overclock.
* `Burn` (2 Unpowered damage to the owner at turn end in hand, `C/Burn.cs:18`) and `Void` (Ethereal, `AfterCardDrawn` -1 energy) are Status cards. Every generator puts its Status in the **discard** pile: `BoostAway` 1 `Dazed`, `GunkUp` 1 `Slimed` (after 3 hits), `FightThrough` 2 `Wound`, `Overclock` draws then 1 `Burn`, `AdaptiveStrike` a 0-cost copy of itself (`SetThisCombat(0)`). `Fuel` is a token (+1 energy); `Compact` transforms every transformable Status in hand into `Fuel` (upgraded if `Compact` is).
* `Ftl`: draws only if finished plays this turn < `PlayMax` (3). `HelixDrill`: hits = energy spent this turn, minus the card's own cost while in the Play pile (`C/HelixDrill.cs`). `FlakCannon`: exhausts every Status not in Exhaust, hits = that count, random targets (`CombatTargets`). `Claw`/`MomentumStrike`/`AdaptiveStrike`: D3; `AllForOne`: all 0-cost non-X cards in discard to hand. `Hologram`: pick a discard card to hand. `GoForTheEyes`: Weak only if the target intends to attack (D2).
* Loadout: `Claw` x3 in deck (all copies grow, including those in the draw pile), upgraded and not; Statuses in hand for `Compact`/`FlakCannon`; `HelixDrill` after spending 3 energy; `Ftl` as the 1st and as the 4th play; `Hologram` with an empty discard.
* Special enemies: `BeamCell` (Vulnerable) and `GoForTheEyes` (Weak) on `cubex_construct_normal` (Artifact blocks the debuff, the damage still lands) and on an enemy mid-attack vs one that is not.

### Batch 5: energy, draw, kill checks, simple powers (16 cards, 3 powers = 19)
Buffer, Synthesis + `FreePowerPower`, MachineLearning + `MachineLearningPower`, SignalBoost + `SignalBoostPower`, GeneticAlgorithm, Reboot, RocketPunch, Scavenge, Scrape, Skim, Sunder, SweepingBeam, Supercritical, Turbo, Uproar, WhiteNoise.
* `Buffer` applies the already ported `BufferPower`. `FreePowerPower`: next `Amount` Power cards cost 0 while in Hand/Play (`P/FreePowerPower.cs`). `SignalBoost`: the next `Amount` **Power** cards play twice (`SignalBoostPower` tests `CardType.Power`, not Skill; `ModifyCardPlayCount`, decrement in `AfterModifyingCardPlayCount`). `MachineLearning`: `ModifyHandDraw` +`Amount`.
* `Sunder`: gains `Energy` if **any** result `WasTargetKilled`; no Fatal/Minion check (`C/Sunder.cs:33-38`). `Scrape`: draw, then discard the drawn cards whose cost is not 0 (X counts as non-zero). `Reboot`: whole hand to the draw pile, shuffle, draw. `Uproar`: 2 hits, then a random draw-pile Attack (non-Unplayable first, else any Attack) via `StableShuffle` on `Shuffle`, auto-played (`C/Uproar.cs`). `WhiteNoise`: a random Defect Power via `CombatCardGeneration`, free this turn. `Scavenge`: exhaust a hand card, `EnergyNextTurn`. `Turbo` adds a `Void` to the discard pile.
* Loadout: `Sunder` on a low-HP enemy (kill) and a high-HP one; `Uproar` with no Attacks, only Unplayable Attacks, and several; `Reboot` with an empty draw pile; `Synthesis` + 2 Powers; `SignalBoost` + Power cards; `GeneticAlgorithm` twice in one fight then in the next fight (D3).
* Special enemies: `Sunder` on `the_kin_boss` Minion follower (still pays energy; contrast with Feed/Fatal, `ironclad.md` §2) and on `fogmog_normal`'s `EyeWithTeeth` (Illusion: the kill revives it, `semantics/monsters.md:52-53`).

### Batch 6: replay, generation and the last relic (6 cards, 6 powers, 1 relic = 13)
CreativeAi + `CreativeAiPower`, EchoForm + `EchoFormPower`, Feral + `FeralPower`, Iteration + `IterationPower`, Smokestack + `SmokestackPower`, Subroutine + `SubroutinePower`; relic `PowerCell`.
* `EchoForm`: the first `Amount` first-in-series plays each turn get +1 play (`P/EchoFormPower.cs`, counts `CardPlayStarted` this turn). `Feral`: the first `Amount` zero-energy Attacks each turn return to the top of the hand; per-turn counter is power data (`P/FeralPower.cs`, also seeded from history in `AfterApplied`, reset `AfterSideTurnStart`). `Iteration`: draws `Amount` on the first Status **drawn** each turn. `Smokestack`: damage to all enemies per Status generated by the owner. `Subroutine`: energy per Power card played. `CreativeAi`: a random Power into hand at `BeforeHandDraw` (`CombatCardGeneration`). `PowerCell`: turn 1, 2 zero-cost cards from the draw pile into the hand (`CombatCardSelection`, `BeforeSideTurnStart`).
* Loadout: `EchoForm` + a draw/energy card, then a 2nd card (only the first is doubled); `Feral` + 0-cost Attacks (`Claw`, `BeamCell`) incl. one already played before `Feral` landed; `Iteration` + a Status drawn twice in a turn (`Void`, `Burn`); `Smokestack` + `Overclock`/`GunkUp`; `PowerCell` with fewer than 2 zero-cost cards in the draw pile.

## 4. Defect mechanics a porter must know
* **Slots/orbs** (`orbs.md` §2-3): 3 base slots (`Models.Characters/Defect.cs:63`), a full queue evokes the front orb on `Channel`; `Capacity == 0` loses the orb. **Focus** enters only through `ModifyOrbValue` (`P/FocusPower.cs:14`); Dark's evoke starts at flat 6 and grows by the Focus-modified passive; Plasma's values are not Focus-modified; Glass decays 1 per passive.
* **Lightning** draws one `CombatTargets` value per random hit, even with one enemy, none with no hittable enemy (`Rng.cs:289-299`). **Dualcast/Shatter/MultiCast** use `evoke(dequeue = false)` repeats (`OC:125-153`).
* **Passive trigger order** at turn end: `Hailstorm` (`BeforeSideTurnEnd`), then orb passives oldest first, then the Ethereal/hand flush, then `ConsumingShadow`/temporary-Focus removal (`AfterSideTurnEnd`). Plasma triggers at turn start after `AfterSideTurnStart`.
* **Cost edits**: `SetThisCombat`/`AddThisCombat` persist for the combat (`MomentumStrike`, `Modded`); `AddUntilPlayed` clears on play (`RocketPunch`); `SetToFreeThisTurn` (`WhiteNoise`) zeroes both energy and star cost.
* **Rng streams**: `CombatOrbs` (`Chaos`, `TrashToTreasure`), `CombatTargets` (Lightning, `FlakCannon`), `CombatCardGeneration` (`WhiteNoise`, `CreativeAi`), `Shuffle` (`Uproar`, `Reboot`), `CombatCardSelection` (`PowerCell`).
* Hibernate (Frost orb multiplayer branch, `FrostOrb.cs:39-49`) is not ported.
