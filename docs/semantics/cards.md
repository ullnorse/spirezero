# Semantics: cards (keywords, history, simple Necrobinder cards)

Append-only. One fact per bullet, with a decomp reference (`ref/<path>:<line>`) and the task id that found it. Index: `docs/semantics.md`.

## Necrobinder simple cards batch 1 (P2)
- `CardModel.CreateClone` keeps the upgrade level, so `Undeath`'s generated copy is upgraded when the played card is (the copy goes to the discard bottom via `AddGeneratedCardToCombat`; `AddToCombatAndPreview` creates unupgraded cards, so it cannot be used). Matches the referee over 25 seeds with `+1` copies. (P2) `Models.Cards/Undeath.cs`, `Models/CardModel.cs:2182-2193`, `Commands/CardPileCmd.cs:281-300`
- `DrainPower` upgrades via `Where(IsUpgradable).TakeRandom(n, Rng.CombatCardSelection)`: `TakeRandom` copies the list and runs `UnstableShuffle` (Fisher-Yates, one `NextInt` per element after the first) eagerly, then takes the first `n`, so the RNG advance depends on the number of upgradable discard cards, not on `n`. `CardCmd.Upgrade` skips cards whose `CurrentUpgradeLevel >= MaxUpgradeLevel` (`Slimed` has `MaxUpgradeLevel` 0) and does nothing once combat is ending. 25 seeds match. (P2) `Extensions/IEnumerableExtensions.cs:17`, `Extensions/ListExtensions.cs:45`, `Commands/CardCmd.cs:267-285`, `Models/CardModel.cs:782-796`
- `Bury` costs 4, more than the 3 base energy, so loadouts need an `ENERGY_POTION` for the random policy to play it. (P2) `Models.Cards/Bury.cs`
- BLOCKED, engine gaps: Ethereal (`Defile`, `Parse`, `PullFromBelow` history, `Eidolon`) needs the end-of-turn exhaust in `DoTurnEnd`; Retain (`Reap`, `Sow`, `Eradicate`, `Wisp` upgrade adds it) needs the `FlushPlayerHand` exemption and per-card keywords; `Eradicate` needs X-cost; `Sow` needs `AllEnemies` targeting; `DeathMarch` and `PullFromBelow` need combat history entries (`CardDrawnEntry` with `FromHandDraw`, `CardPlayFinishedEntry.WasEthereal`); `Eidolon` needs `CardCmd.AutoPlay` and a cost upgrade. (P2) the card files under `Models.Cards/`

## Card keywords and combat history (C5)
Paths are under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`.
- A card's `Keywords` are its local set (canonical, plus `AddKeyword`, minus `RemoveKeyword`; copied by clones) plus global ones from `Hook.ModifyKeywordsInCombat` (no Act 1 listener; `HexPower` adds Ethereal to its owner's Hexed cards; global keywords are computed on each read, never stored, so they vanish with the listener). Upgrade-time keyword and cost changes happen in `OnUpgrade`, so a combat card cloned from an upgraded deck card carries them. (C5) `Models/CardModel.cs:499-526,1153-1182,1330-1342`; hook (XC24) `Hooks/Hook.cs:1603`, `Models.Powers/HexPower.cs`
- Eidolon's `CanonicalKeywords` is only Exhaust; the Ethereal the inventory lists comes from its hover tip. (C5) `Models.Cards/Eidolon.cs`
- `FlushPlayerHand` keeps every `ShouldRetainThisTurn` card (Retain keyword or single-turn retain), then runs `EndOfTurnCleanup` on every card in every combat pile: clears `ExhaustOnNextPlay`, single-turn Retain/Sly and turn-scoped cost modifiers. `EndEnemyTurnInternal` runs the same cleanup between `BeforeSideTurnEnd` and `AfterSideTurnEnd`. (C5) `Combat/CombatManager.cs:1785-1818`, `Entities.Players/PlayerCombatState.cs:268`, `Models/CardModel.cs:1611`
- Innate (turn 1 only): each Innate draw-pile card, in pile order, is moved to the top, so the last one ends up first; the hand draw becomes `min(max(handDraw, innateCount), 10)`. (C5) `Combat/CombatManager.cs:906-919`, `Entities.Cards/CardPile.cs:160`
- Before that, each draw-pile card whose enchantment `ShouldStartAtBottomOfDrawPile` (only `Imbued`), in pile order, moves to the bottom (`MoveToBottomInternal`), and those cards are left out of the Innate move and count even if Innate. (PX13) `Combat/CombatManager.cs:905-917`, `Entities.Cards/CardPile.cs:142-150`, `Models.Enchantments/Imbued.cs:11`
- X cost: `Canonical` is 0 and `GetWithModifiers` returns the base for X costs, so `CanPlay` always has enough energy; `GetAmountToSpend` is all current energy, captured as `CapturedXValue` in `SpendEnergy` (`EnergySpent` history entry only if > 0). `ResolveEnergyXValue` runs `Hook.ModifyXValue` on the captured value. (C5) `Entities.Cards/CardEnergyCost.cs`, `Models/CardModel.cs:1117-1124,1807-1834`
- Local cost modifiers apply in insertion order (`Absolute` replaces, `Relative` adds; `reduceOnly` takes the min), before the global hooks, then `max(0, ..)`. Until-played modifiers are removed at the very end of `OnPlayWrapper` (after `CheckForEmptyHand`); `UpgradeBy(n)` lowers any absolute modifier above the new base. (C5) `Entities.Cards/CardEnergyCost.cs`, `Entities.Cards/LocalCostModifier.cs`, `Models/CardModel.cs:2007`
- `CardCmd.AutoPlay`: no-op if combat is over/ending or the owner is dead; an Unplayable card or a `ShouldPlay` veto goes to the Play pile and then to Exhaust (Exhaust keyword or `ExhaustOnNextPlay`) or Discard, never played (a power card is discarded too). A null `AnyEnemy` target is `Rng.CombatTargets.NextItem(HittableEnemies)`. X-cost cards capture current energy without spending it; then `OnPlayWrapper(isAutoPlay)` adds the card to the Play pile with a normal `CardPileCmd.Add`. (C5) `Commands/CardCmd.cs:51-137`, `Models/CardModel.cs:2101-2121`
- An autoplayed Ethereal card without the Exhaust keyword (e.g. Defile from Eidolon) ends in the discard pile: Ethereal only exhausts at turn end in hand. (C5) `Models/CardModel.cs:2084-2096`
- `CardPlayFinishedEntry.WasEthereal` is read when the entry is logged, after `OnPlay`; `CombatHistory` is cleared after `AfterCombatEnd`, before `AfterCombatVictory`. `HappenedThisTurn` compares round, side and each player's turn number. (C5) `Combat.History.Entries/CardPlayFinishedEntry.cs`, `Combat/CombatManager.cs:1315-1316`, `Combat.History/CombatHistoryEntry.cs`
- `CardModel.CreateClone` copies the card (upgrade level, keywords, energy-cost state) without `ExhaustOnNextPlay` and runs no `AfterCreated`; Undeath then adds it with `AddGeneratedCardToCombat`, which logs `CardGenerated` and fires `AfterCardGeneratedForCombat`. (C5) `Models/CardModel.cs:2182-2193`, `Commands/CardPileCmd.cs:267-313`
- `TargetingAllOpponents` re-reads `GetOpponentsOf(attacker)` before every hit; `WithHitCount(0)` still fires `BeforeAttack`/`AfterAttack` and logs `CreatureAttacked`. (C5) `Commands.Builders/AttackCommand.cs:163-181,526-674`

## Necrobinder cards batch P4
- `OstyDamageVar` cards (`Poke`, `Fetch`, `Flatten`, `BoneShards`) attack with Osty as the attacker (`FromOsty`), so the player's Strength does not apply, and do nothing at all (no draw, no block) when `Owner.IsOstyMissing` (Osty absent or dead). `BoneShards` kills Osty after the block if it is still alive. (P4) `Models.Cards/Poke.cs`, `Models.Cards/BoneShards.cs`, `Models/Player.cs` (`IsOstyAlive`)
- `Flatten` listens to `AfterAttack` (attacker is Osty) and `AfterCardEnteredCombat` (Osty already attacked this turn) and sets `EnergyCost.SetThisTurn(0)`; `Fetch` draws only on the first finished play of that card instance per turn (`HasBeenPlayedThisTurn` reads `CardPlaysFinished`, the current play is not finished yet). (P4) `Models.Cards/Flatten.cs`, `Models.Cards/Fetch.cs`
- `BansheesCry` and `Melancholy` lower their own cost for the combat as listeners (`AfterCardPlayed` of an Ethereal card, `AfterCardEnteredCombat` counting finished Ethereal plays; `AfterDeath` of any creature not removal-prevented). `BansheesCry` skips `AfterCardEnteredCombat` for clones (`CloneOf != null`, set only by `CardModel.CreateClone`); the engine's `Card` has no clone flag, so a cloned Banshee's Cry (via Undeath) would be wrong until that is added. (P4) `Models.Cards/BansheesCry.cs`, `Models.Cards/Melancholy.cs`, `Models/CardModel.cs:698-703,2182-2193`
- `HangPower` is a counter debuff multiplying (`ModifyDamageMultiplicative`) damage whose card source is a `Hang` card; the card applies `max(2, current amount)` each play, so the power doubles. `Misery` snapshots the target's debuffs (`TypeForCurrentAmount == Debuff`) before its hit, then stacks or applies each non-zero one on every other hittable enemy with the original applier. (P4) `Models.Powers/HangPower.cs`, `Models.Cards/Hang.cs`, `Models.Cards/Misery.cs`

## Afflictions, enchantments, transform, downgrade, discard (C6)
Paths are under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`.
- `CardCmd.Afflict` does nothing for a combat-pile card once combat is over or ending, on a `ShouldAfflict` veto or when `CanAfflict` fails (card type, Unplayable, an existing affliction unless stackable and the same kind); otherwise it sets the affliction (or adds to the same kind's amount) and logs `CardAfflicted`. `ClearAffliction` just removes it. A clone keeps the affliction and the enchantment. (C6) `Commands/CardCmd.cs:617-673`, `Models/AfflictionModel.cs`, `Models/CardModel.cs:1205-1216`
- `TangledPower` afflicts every Attack in all combat piles (Hand, Draw, Discard, Exhaust, Play) with `Entangled` in `AfterApplied` and any new Attack in `AfterCardEnteredCombat`, adds `Amount` in `TryModifyEnergyCostInCombat` for Entangled cards, removes itself at the end of its owner's turn, and clears every `Entangled` in `AfterRemoved`. `Entangled` itself overrides nothing. 30 `VineShamblerNormal` seeds match. (C6) `Models.Powers/TangledPower.cs`, `Models.Afflictions/Entangled.cs`
- `Hook.ModifyDamage` applies the card source's `EnchantDamageAdditive`, then `EnchantDamageMultiplicative`, before the listener passes, and the enchantment is not a modifier. `Vigorous` adds `Amount` to powered attacks until its card's first `AfterCardPlayed` disables it; `Slither` sets its card's cost for the combat to `Rng.CombatEnergyCosts.NextInt(4)` when drawn into the hand. `CanEnchant` rejects Status, Curse and Quest cards. (C6) `Hooks/Hook.cs:1495-1509`, `Models.Enchantments/Vigorous.cs`, `Models.Enchantments/Slither.cs`, `Models/EnchantmentModel.cs:273-294`
- `CardCmd.TransformTo` (one card): nothing once combat is ending; the new card (`CreateCard`, with `AfterCreated`) takes the original's index in its pile, logs `CardGenerated`, fires `AfterCardEnteredCombat` and `AfterCardChangedPiles` with the *same* pile as the old pile, then `AfterCardGeneratedForCombat`; the original leaves the combat with no hook. (C6) `Commands/CardCmd.cs:337-520`
- `CardSelectCmd.FromCombatPile` with a selector (the referee's headless one) lists a draw pile ordered by `Rarity`, then `ModelId` (stable), so `select_cards` indices refer to that order; the choice itself does not depend on it. `Seance` (cost 1, 0 upgraded, Ethereal) transforms its pick into `Soul` (cost 0, Exhaust, draw 2/3). 10 seeds each on two encounters match with `c6-seance.json`. (C6) `Commands/CardSelectCmd.cs:444-495`, `Models.Cards/Seance.cs`, `Models.Cards/Soul.cs`
- `CardCmd.Downgrade` (unless combat is ending) resets the upgrade level, `DynamicVars`, the base cost (`ResetForDowngrade`; modifiers stay) and the local keywords to the canonical ones, then calls `AfterDowngraded`; the affliction, enchantment and the class's own fields stay. `TheScythe` deals `13 + IncreasedDamage` and adds 5 (7 upgraded) after each play; `AfterDowngraded` recomputes `CurrentDamage` from `IncreasedDamage`. (C6) `Commands/CardCmd.cs:212-224`, `Models/CardModel.cs:2142-2156`, `Models.Cards/TheScythe.cs`
- `CardCmd.Discard` (`DiscardAndDraw` with 0): nothing once combat is over or ending; each card goes to the discard pile, logs `CardDiscarded` and fires `AfterCardDiscarded` (guarded) in turn; the cards that were Sly this turn (`Sly` keyword or single-turn Sly) are autoplayed afterwards. (C6) `Commands/CardCmd.cs:147-209`, `Models/CardModel.cs:603-630`

## Necrobinder cards batch P5
Paths are under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`.
- `SculptingStrike` and `Snap` have no `CanonicalKeywords` (the Ethereal / Retain in the inventory is a hover tip, like `Eidolon`); each hits, then picks one hand card (`FromHand`, exactly 1, filter: no local Ethereal / no Retain) and `CardCmd.ApplyKeyword`s it for the rest of the combat. `Snap` still offers the pick when Osty is missing. (P5) `Models.Cards/SculptingStrike.cs`, `Models.Cards/Snap.cs`

Any later-ported card with the tag must override it. `Squeeze` counts every other tagged card in all five combat piles. (P5) `Models.Cards/Squeeze.cs`
- `Protector` scales with Osty's *max* HP (`10 + 1 × MaxHp`, cost drops to 0 upgraded); `Sacrifice` computes `3 × MaxHp` block, then kills Osty, then gains the block; `Rattle` hits `1 + (CreatureAttacked entries by Osty this turn)` times. (P5) `Models.Cards/Protector.cs`, `Models.Cards/Sacrifice.cs`, `Models.Cards/Rattle.cs`
- `Dredge` takes `min(3, 10 - hand size)` cards from the discard pile (exact pick count, so a smaller pile gives all of it); `Graveblast` returns 1 discard card to hand after the hit; `Cleanse` summons first, then exhausts 1 draw-pile card. All are `FromCombatPile` (exact count, no choice when the pile is no larger than the count). (P5) `Models.Cards/Dredge.cs`, `Models.Cards/Graveblast.cs`, `Models.Cards/Cleanse.cs`
- BLOCKED (engine): `Transfigure` needs `BaseReplayCount` (a per-card replay count feeding `GeneratePlayCount`) and a hand pick; `RightHandHand` needs `CardPlay.Resources.EnergyValue` (energy spent by the played card, `GetAmountToSpend` for an autoplay) in `after_card_played_late`. Neither exists on `CardPlay` / `Card`. (P5) `Models.Cards/Transfigure.cs`, `Models.Cards/RightHandHand.cs`, `Commands/CardCmd.cs:126`

## Necrobinder cards batch P6
Paths are under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`.
- `DoomPower` kills at `BeforeSideTurnEnd` (side != Player, i.e. enemies at the end of the enemy turn) and `AfterSideTurnEnd` (side != Enemy, players): only the first doomed creature of the side (`HP <= Amount`, in `GetCreaturesOnSide` order) triggers `DoomKill` for all of them, each killed in turn via `CreatureCmd.Kill`, then one `Hook.AfterDiedToDoom` with the list. The Vfx waits have no game effect. `BlightStrike` applies Doom equal to the sum of `BlockedDamage + UnblockedDamage` of its hit. (P6) `Models.Powers/DoomPower.cs`, `Models.Cards/BlightStrike.cs`, `Entities.Creatures/DamageResult.cs:63`
- `Weak` is `0.75m` damage for its owner's powered attacks; `Vulnerable` is `1.5m` for its owner. `DebilitatePower` on the owner doubles the effect: Weak `0.75 - (1 - 0.75) = 0.5`, Vulnerable `1.5 + (1.5 - 1) = 2.0`. It decrements after every side's turn end that contains its owner. `PaperKrane`/`PaperPhrog`/`CrueltyPower` tweaks stay unported. (P6) `Models.Powers/WeakPower.cs`, `Models.Powers/VulnerablePower.cs`, `Models.Powers/DebilitatePower.cs`
- `EnfeeblingTouchPower` is a `TemporaryStrengthPower` with `IsPositive` false: `BeforeApplied` applies `-amount` Strength (silent), a stack applies `-delta` more in `AfterPowerAmountChanged` (skipped when the change equals the whole `Amount`, i.e. the first application), and at its owner's side's `AfterSideTurnEnd` it removes itself and applies `+Amount` Strength. (P6) `Models.Powers/TemporaryStrengthPower.cs`
- `LethalityPower` multiplies a powered Attack card's damage by `1 + Amount/100` when no other Attack card was started this turn (the played card counts itself when it is in the Play pile) and its `CurrentPlayIndex` is 0; the engine reads the index from the card's latest `CardPlayStarted` history entry. `DanseMacabrePower` gains `Amount` unpowered Block in `BeforeCardPlayed` when the card's resolved cost (captured X for X costs) is at least 2. `BorrowedTimePower` adds `Amount` to every card's cost and removes itself after its owner's side's turn. `DemesnePower` adds `Amount` to hand draw and max energy. `DeathsDoor` gains its block once, plus 2 more times if a `PowerReceivedEntry` for Doom applied by the player happened this turn. (P6) `Models.Powers/LethalityPower.cs`, `Models.Powers/DanseMacabrePower.cs`, `Models.Powers/BorrowedTimePower.cs`, `Models.Powers/DemesnePower.cs`, `Models.Cards/DeathsDoor.cs`
- `CaptureSpirit` adds its Souls with `CardPilePosition.Random` (ported in C10). `CallOfTheVoid` is ported in the game as a `BeforeHandDraw` generator over the whole Necrobinder pool; `get_distinct_for_combat` panics on any unported card, so it can only be added once the pool is fully ported. (P6) `Models.Cards/CaptureSpirit.cs`, `Models.Powers/CallOfTheVoidPower.cs`

## Card play gaps C10
Paths are under `ref/decomp/MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.`.
- `CardPilePosition.Random` in `CardPileCmd.Add` draws `Rng.Shuffle.NextInt(cardPile.Cards.Count + 1)` per card, after the card left its old pile and against the pile it actually goes to (a full hand redirects to the discard pile); the draw happens even for an empty pile (bound 1). `AddGeneratedCardsToCombat` is per card `History.CardGenerated`, `Add(card, pile, position)`, `AfterCardGeneratedForCombat`, over a list built beforehand (`Soul.Create` makes every Soul first, so `AfterCreated` for all precedes the first add). `AutoPlayFromDrawPile` with `Random` picks on `Rng.CombatCardSelection`. (C10) `Commands/CardPileCmd.cs:267-313,509,1163`, `Models.Cards/Soul.cs`
- Play count is `GetEnchantedReplayCount() + 1` (`BaseReplayCount`, a plain field that survives `CreateClone`) before `ModifyCardPlayCount`. `Transfigure` adds 1 to the energy cost for the combat only when `!CostsX` and the base cost is >= 0, and always `BaseReplayCount++`. (C10) `Models/CardModel.cs:396,1132,2029`, `Models.Cards/Transfigure.cs`
- `CardPlay.Resources.EnergyValue`: a manual play's energy spent (`GetAmountToSpend()`, X = all energy); an auto-play spends 0 but `EnergyValue` is `GetAmountToSpend()` after the X capture. `RightHandHand` (cost 0, Osty 4/6) returns itself from the discard pile to the hand in `AfterCardPlayedLate` when `EnergyValue >= 2` (for any played card; the card itself is still in the Play pile during its own play). `CaptureSpirit` deals unblockable unpowered damage straight through `CreatureCmd.Damage` with itself as source (3/4, dealer = owner); `Reave` upgrades its Soul first when upgraded; `Severance` makes 3 Souls: draw (Random), discard, hand. (C10) `GameActions/PlayCardAction.cs:92-100`, `Commands/CardCmd.cs:118-130`, `Models.Cards/RightHandHand.cs`, `Models.Cards/CaptureSpirit.cs`, `Models.Cards/Reave.cs`, `Models.Cards/Severance.cs`

## Necrobinder Doom/Soul/Osty power cards (P8)
- `OblivionPower` is `InstancedPerApplier`, but `apply_power` finds an existing instance by kind only; the player is the only applier in scope, so the two are equivalent. Its `amountsForPlayedCards` dictionary (card -> amount at `BeforeCardPlayed`, removed in `AfterCardPlayed`, which applies that much Doom) is a fixed 8-slot array in `PowerData::Oblivion` (nested `AutoPlay`s are the only way entries overlap). (P8) `Models.Powers/{Countdown,Haunt,NecroMastery,ReaperForm,Calcify}Power.cs`
- None of the P8 powers or cards carries `ITemporaryPower` or the `OstyAttack` tag. `Dirge` runs `OstyCmd.Summon` X times, then creates X Souls (upgraded when Dirge is) and shuffles them into the draw pile at `Position::Random`. (P8) `Models.Cards/Dirge.cs`

## Curses, status, event and quest cards batch P11
- `Doubt` and `Shame` apply Weak or Frail to the owner at `OnTurnEndInHand` with a null applier; their `SkipNextDurationTick = true` on a new power is already the rule `PowerCmd.Apply` runs for every debuff on a player (`target.Side == Player && Type == Debuff`), so the card adds nothing. (P11) `Models.Cards/Doubt.cs`, `Commands/PowerCmd.cs:148`
- `Regret` snapshots `Pile.Cards.Count` (the whole hand, itself included) in `BeforeSideTurnEnd` while it is in hand, and deals that much unblockable, unpowered damage at `OnTurnEndInHand`, then resets the count; the engine keeps it in `CardData::Regret`. Other end-of-turn-in-hand cards and Ethereal exhausts have already left the hand by `OnTurnEndInHand`, hence the snapshot. (P11) `Models.Cards/Regret.cs`
