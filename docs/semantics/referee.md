# Semantics: referee harness

Append-only. One fact per bullet, with a decomp reference (`ref/<path>:<line>`) and the task id that found it. Index: `docs/semantics.md`.

## Referee harness (T3)
Details in `referee/README.md`. Paths under `ref/decomp/` for game facts; the rest are observations of the referee itself.
- An Act 1 run's normal fights are pre-rolled by `ActModel.GenerateRooms` (weak pool first, `AddWithoutRepeatingTags` over a `GrabBag`), so the first fight is a function of seed plus `UnlockState`. Seed `T3S0` gives `FuzzyWurmCrawlerWeak` first (Overgrowth); fresh-process runs of it give HP 57 and the move cycle `FIRST_ACID_GOOP, INHALE, ACID_GOOP, FIRST_ACID_GOOP`. (T3) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Models/ActModel.cs:331-386`
- On a profile with `NumberOfRuns == 0` Overgrowth overrides the order (Nibbits, Slimes, ShrinkerBeetle weak first), so the first fight is not seed-determined there. The real profile has about 230 runs, and the referee also shows a seed-dependent order, so it is not in that mode. (T3) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Models.Acts/Overgrowth.cs:110-122`
- Combat RNG streams are untouched by Neow: entering the first fight directly gives the same shuffle, hand and monster HP as going through Neow with a deck-neutral choice. (T3, observed)
- Necrobinder turn 1 on `T3S0`: 66 HP, 3 energy, Osty alive with 1 HP and `DIE_FOR_YOU_POWER` 1, hand 5, draw 5; `draw[0]` is the top of the pile. (T3, observed)
- Inputs beyond the seed: character, ascension, `UnlockState` (epoch reveals filter event pools; `NumberOfRuns`, seen encounters affect room order). The harness pins `UnlockState.all` and reads no save profile. (T3)


## Run-mode prompts (RL3)
- sts2-cli runs an event option on a background task. When the option asks for cards (Neow's Pomander, Precise Scissors, Precarious Shears), `select_cards` used to return before the task applied the pick, so its trace line showed the event still open and the deck unchanged. The change only appeared one line later. `referee/setup.sh` now patches `DoSelectCards` to wait for the event task, as sts2-cli already does for Smith. (RL3, observed on seeds `RL2S1`, `RL2S5`, `RL2S6`) `referee/vendor/sts2-cli/src/Sts2Headless/RunSimulator.cs` `DoSelectCards`
- sts2-cli auto-claims a non-card reward set from an event or relic (`SmallCapsule`) in `HeadlessOfferAsync`, in sorted reward order. A combat's gold, potion and relic rewards are claimed in `DetectPostCombatState` before the card reward is shown. `odds` print as the shortest round-trip `float` (`-1`, `0.089999996`). (RL3) `RunSimulator.cs` `RewardsSetOfferPrefix`, `DetectPostCombatState`


## Parity hardening (T7)
- From ascension 2 the forced-encounter run starts at 52 of 66 HP (`hp`/`max_hp` at step 0; observed at A2, A3, A4 and A10, `StartRun` passes the level and nothing in `RunSimulator.cs` sets the HP; the cause was not found in the decomp). The engine starts at full HP. (T7, observed) Pinned as `a10_start_hp`.
- From ascension 5 (`AscensionLevel.AscendersBane`) the starting deck has an `ASCENDERS_BANE` (unplayable, eternal), so the draw pile has 11 cards and the shuffle makes one more `Shuffle` draw; the engine has no such card. (T7) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Entities.Ascension/AscensionManager.cs:60`. Pinned as `a10_ascenders_bane`. A loadout `deck` replaces the starting deck, which is how `sts2-diff gen` keeps A5+ runs comparable.
- Ascension 4 and above (`TightBelt`) leave 2 potion slots, so a loadout of 3 potions is rejected there (hence the `"ascensions"` key of a loadout). (T7) `AscensionManager.cs:56`
- `start_run` draws about 410 times from the `UpFront` stream (map and room generation) before the fight, so a combat-only engine session compares that counter as draws since step 0. Every other stream matches by absolute count at step 0 (`Shuffle` 9 on the starter deck). (T7, observed)
- After a won fight the dump shows the torn-down room: every pile emptied (`PlayerCombatState.AfterCombatEnd`), the player's powers and block removed (`Player.AfterCombatEnd`), Osty gone, energy untouched, and the potion reward already in the belt (sts2-cli claims it). After a lost fight nothing is torn down: piles, powers and Osty stay as they were. (T7) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Combat/CombatManager.cs:1315-1325`, `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Entities.Players/Player.cs:837`
- The teardown does not clear `CombatState.Enemies`: the dump of a won fight lists whatever was never removed, normally nothing (dead enemies leave on death), but the dead Eye With Teeth (`IllusionPower.ShouldCreatureBeRemovedFromCombatAfterDeath` is false for its owner) stays when the Fogmog dies first. The harness's `state_json` therefore lists the engine's enemy list on a win too. (F1) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Combat/CombatManager.cs:1297-1360`, `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Models.Powers/IllusionPower.cs:108`; fixture `fogmog_won_eye_stays`
- Card `Keywords` in the dump are `CardModel.Keywords` (local plus `ModifyKeywordsInCombat`); `retain`/`sly` are `ShouldRetainThisTurn`/`IsSlyThisTurn`, which fold in the single-turn flags that the engine keeps as `single_turn_retain`/`single_turn_sly`. (T7) `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Models/CardModel.cs:526-630`

## TestMode (R5, full audit in `docs/specs/test-mode.md`)
- sts2-cli sets `TestMode.IsOn = true` once, in `EnsureModelDbInitialized` (`RunSimulator.cs:4163`). Of the 322 `IsOn` and 64 `IsOff` uses in the decomp, 7 change gameplay: shop potion price roll (`MerchantPotionEntry.cs:67`, modelled), Cauldron's fixed 5 potions (`Cauldron.cs:40`) and CallingBell's fixed 3 relics (`CallingBell.cs:39`) instead of `Rewards`/relic rolls, tutorial modifications (`RunManager.cs:757,773,1003`: first-run/undiscovered-content only), bundle choice always bundle 0 (`CardSelectCmd.cs:978`), and the auto-claim in `RewardsSet.cs:172`. Everything else is UI, audio, VFX, saves or telemetry.
- The referee cannot run with TestMode off: `start_run` fails in `RunManager.FadeOut` (null `NTransition`) before the map, and the ~240 `Nodes.*` hits need a Godot scene tree. Tried with a throwaway copy of the build (`TestMode.IsOn = false` behind an env var); nothing committed. A targeted `Mono.Cecil` IL patch of one `IsOff` site is the only viable way to match the real game for a single branch.
- sts2-cli builds its run with `RunState.CreateForTest` and never calls `ActModel.GetRandomList`, so act selection is not affected by the `IsOff` check at `ActModel.cs:550`.

## Tooling (F5)
- `tools/verify_main.sh` piped `regress.sh` into `tail -1`, which prints only when the pipeline ends. A fresh `~/.cache/spirezero-verify` worktree has an empty regress cache, so the run takes 30 to 45 minutes (1342 runs at 5 seeds), and a caller that stopped waiting saw no line. It now logs to `/tmp/verify-regress.log`, shares the main checkout's content-addressed cache (`REGRESS_CACHE`) and always prints the `regress:` line, or says it found none. Editing `regress.sh` while it runs breaks the running copy (bash reads the script lazily), as seen in F5. (F5, observed)
- The tracer dumps `player.stars` (`PlayerCombatState.Stars`) for every character and `osty` as `{"alive": false}` when `player.Osty` is null (every non-Necrobinder); the engine's trace now emits both, so `stars` is compared (0 for non-Regents). `referee.py` already starts the actions file's `character`; `sts2-diff gen/run-gen --character NAME` and `CHARACTER=Ironclad tools/diff.sh` pass it. (C15) `referee/tracer/TraceDump.cs:40-60`

## Final-matrix flakes (PX15)
- XT5's 10-seed `--acts both` divergences (23 seeds over 7 cells) were all one harness fault: each candidate's `referee_trace` was empty and its actions matched today's. A slot daemon inherits the environment of the job that spawned it, including `REFEREE_STATS` in that checkout; once the checkout was deleted, `Game.__init__`'s `trace_cache.note("game")` raised `FileNotFoundError`, so every job that missed the store failed before the game started (`fail referee error: FileNotFoundError`, 0 lines) until the daemon idled out. XT5 made `note` best effort (8177679); the pre-fix `trace_cache.py` reproduces it with `REFEREE_STATS` in a missing directory. (PX15, observed)
- `run-gen --real-game 1` wrote `real_shop_prices` and `real_cauldron` but not `real_calling_bell`, so Darv's `CallingBell` gave the referee TestMode's fixed relics while the engine (out of test mode) rolled them (`RL2S114`, real-shop cell). Engine side was right (`content/relics/calling_bell.rs`, `Models.Relics/CallingBell.cs:39`); fixture `darv_calling_bell_real_relics`. (PX15)
- sts2-cli's run-mode waits on a background game task (event option, shop purchase/removal, rest option, run loadout) gave up after a fixed time (`task.Wait(2000)`, 2000 polls after an event pick or reward, a fixed 200 ms after a shop removal) and dumped the state as it stood. On the saturated box of the 200-seed matrix, `SelfHelpBook`'s enchant (`SelectAndEnchant`, no delay in the game) had not run when `select_cards` returned: trace `event_choice`, deck unenchanted (`RL2S35`, Necrobinder overgrowth shopper A10; deterministic on rerun). `setup.sh` now keeps the event and shop tasks and waits through `RunWaits.cs` `WaitForGameTask`/`WaitForEventOption` (the old conditions, `PumpUntil`'s 30 s, a timeout is an error). (PX15) `Models.Events/SelfHelpBook.cs:104-114`
- A shop relic whose purchase offers rewards that do not all fit (`Cauldron`'s 5 potions on a full belt, F3 above) now completes: the selector takes what fits, skips the rest, and the purchase restocks (a `TheCourier` roll) or clears the slot, runs `AfterItemPurchased` and `UpdateEntries`. Both now take the real game's path. Fixture `cauldron_bought_then_card_removal` (`ROT261019S135` Ironclad, Hive act jump, Cauldron then a card removal). (PX15; fixed in RF2's follow-up)

- The same mid-play end of turn breaks sts2-cli when the next turn's start opens a choice (`EntropyPower`'s transform): `DoPlayCard` → `DetectDecisionPoint` → `SettledCombatDecision` does not see the prompt and `CombatPlayState` throws `InvalidOperationException: Collection was modified` enumerating the hand (`RunSimulator.cs:2646`, stack trace from the error's `stack_trace`, which `referee.py` drops). Harness artifact, not the game: the diff policy also refuses a `VoidForm` whose play leaves a pending choice (`referee_broken_play`); test `a_void_form_that_opens_a_next_turn_choice_is_refused`. (PX20, Necrobinder A10 `ROT261023S3`)

## Runtime independence (PX16)
- sts2-cli's runtime Harmony patches (`Task.Yield`, `Cmd.Wait`, `TalkCmd.Play`, `SoulNexus.AfterDeath`, `SandpitPower`, `RewardsSet.Offer`, `FromChooseABundleScreen`, the `LocTable`/`LocString` fallbacks) applied or not depending on the installed .NET: the project rolls forward (`RollForward=LatestMajor`), and on .NET 10 Harmony fails (`CoreCLR version 10.0.13 is not supported`, the RL4 note above), so every parity result so far is the game without them.
